How Blink Operates? Business model & Technology

Intro: The Financial Technology (fintech) industry has taken the global economy by storm in the recent years. Mobile financial apps and online payments are becoming more relevant compared to traditional cash transactions. In Mauritius, the development of fintech products has recently surged, with the introduction of the MAUCAS QR and IPS switch by the Bank of Mauritius, it enabled companies to develop instant payment technologies. Emtel, a Mauritian telecommunications and network company has also decided to join the Fintech Market in May 2022, when it launched its app named Blink. Blink enables citizens to seamlessly do interbank transfers, MAUCAS QR payments, pay merchants, pay bills, do peer to peer transfers all within the same app. Although Blink was not the first of its kind in the island, it did bring some innovations with it such as the dynamic qr code and cross-platform interoperability.

P1: Blink is a digital payment application which has integrated the Bank of Mauritius’ IPS switch and MAUCAS QR infrastructure. Blink does not operate like traditional digital wallets; it does not hold any customer funds. Instead, transactions are made directly between customers’ linked bank accounts. This reduces risk for Blink as funds remain with regulated banking institutions at all times. When registering for Blink, individuals go through mobile number authentication and Know Your Client (KYC) verification such as ID and proof of address verification. This ensures compliance with policies and AML: anti money laundering regulations.
From a business perspective, Blink generates revenue through commission fees charged on transactions, merchant service charges and partnerships with other companies. The profitability of Blink depends heavily on transaction volumes and merchant turnovers. While Emtel is continuously innovating and scaling Blink, it is also important to note that Mauritius has a rather small population, and this may limit the scalability of Blink. Strategic ecosystem integration is what is necessary to make Blink become a more valuable product. Innovations like an ecommerce checkout payment gateway can help blink get more transactions and therefore increasing profitability.
